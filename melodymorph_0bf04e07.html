    UNDER THE HOOD

    How MelodyMorph hears music

    🎚️

    STEP 1

    Source separation

    A Demucs/Spleeter-style stage splits a mix into vocals, piano/guitar, bass & drums. Solo a stem above to hear the idea.

    🧠

    STEP 2

    Music transcription

    Basic Pitch-style AMT estimates pitch (autocorrelation in Web Audio), onset time, duration & velocity for every note.

    ⌗

    STEP 3

    Quantize & understand

    Notes snap to a grid, BPM comes from onset intervals, key from a Krumhansl-Schmuckler profile.

    🎹

    STEP 4

    Play, swap, export

    Built-in synths replay the score on 8 instruments. Export a standard MIDI file or printable sheet music.
